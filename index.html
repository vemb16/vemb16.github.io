Theoretical Quantum and Classical Computation

Hello!
I recently graduated from EPFL (École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne) with a Master's degree in Computer and Quantum Sciences, focusing on theoretical research.

I am passionate about exploring the mathematical foundations of Computer Science and Physics, particularly theoretical quantum and classical computation. My research has included classical computational complexity (restricted variants of MCSP), as well as building quantum circuits for a restricted variant of the quantum Schur transform, using representation theory and combinatorics.

Beyond research, I enjoy learning, challenging myself and sharing knowledge. I work as a private mathematics tutor and create educational videos on YouTube, where I try to make complex topics fun, intuitive and accessible to any curious mind.

I am currently looking for a PhD position starting around September 2027, in theoretical computer science, particularly computational complexity and the mathematical foundations of computation, both classical and quantum.
